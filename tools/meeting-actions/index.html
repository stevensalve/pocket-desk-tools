1. Your notes

What does it look for?
  • Lines starting with Action:, TODO:, Follow-up: or a checkbox [ ].
  • Bullets under a heading such as Actions or Next steps.
  • Sentences like Sam will call the vendor (shown as a lower-confidence guess).
  • Owners: @name, owner: Name, a trailing (Name) or Name: task.
  • Dates: by Friday, tomorrow, EOW, 25 Oct, 2026-11-30, in 2 weeks.
  • Decision: / We agreed ... and Q: lines go to their own lists.
